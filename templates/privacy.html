Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2025

This application (the "App") is a private CSV-to-QuickBooks Online integration operated by Gyno Solutions (Nairobi, Kenya) for use by a single QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the general public.

What this App does

The App reads a CSV file uploaded by an authorized user, and creates corresponding records (customers, items, invoices, sales receipts) in QuickBooks Online via Intuit's official API.

What data we store

  • QuickBooks OAuth credentials. Refresh token, access token, and company ID (realm ID), issued by Intuit when you authorize the App. Stored in an encrypted managed Postgres database.
  • Processing logs. Which invoice numbers were processed, their success or failure status, and any error message returned by QuickBooks. Retained for troubleshooting and re-run safety.

What data we do not store

  • CSV file contents — the uploaded file is processed in memory and deleted immediately after use.
  • Any QuickBooks customer, vendor, or transaction records beyond the invoice number and status noted above.
  • No credit card numbers, no payment information, no patient medical records beyond what is in the CSV the user explicitly uploads.

How we use your data

  • To authenticate with QuickBooks Online on your behalf.
  • To create the records you asked the App to create.
  • To record what was processed so a re-upload does not create duplicates.
  • To diagnose errors when a sync fails.

Third parties

The App shares data only with:

  • Intuit QuickBooks Online, to perform the synchronization you requested.
  • Railway (hosting) and Railway Postgres (database), which host the running application and the credentials listed above.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other party, and we do not use it for marketing or advertising.

Data retention and deletion

  • OAuth credentials are retained while the App is connected to QuickBooks. Disconnecting the App, or revoking access from your Intuit account, invalidates them.
  • Processing logs are retained indefinitely for audit purposes. They can be deleted on request.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, email info@domfin.co.ke.

Security

  • All traffic between the App, your browser, and Intuit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • The Postgres database is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider.
  • Access to the App is restricted to the operator's Railway dashboard.

Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke the App's access from your Intuit account settings.
  • Request a copy of the data the App holds about your QuickBooks connection.
  • Request deletion of the stored data.

To exercise any of these, contact info@domfin.co.ke.
